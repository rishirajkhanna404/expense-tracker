Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendwise collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Spendwise is a personal expense tracking service, and we are committed to handling your data with care and transparency.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic identifying information such as your name and email address, along with a hashed password so you can sign in securely. We never see or store your password in plain text.

As you use Spendwise, we store the expense entries you create — categories, amounts, dates, and any descriptions you choose to add. If you upload or link additional information (such as a profile image), that data is stored alongside your account.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as the fact that you logged in or the time of a request, in order to keep the service secure and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide exclusively to operate Spendwise. This includes displaying your expense history, generating category and time-period summaries, filtering records on your behalf, and letting you export or delete your data when you ask.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your expense entries to build advertising profiles, and we do not share your personal information with marketers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers and is associated with your account via your user ID. We use industry- standard safeguards — including hashed passwords, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and restricted access — to protect your information from unauthorized access.

While no system is perfectly secure, we work continually to keep your data safe. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you promptly.

You may delete your account and all data associated with it at any time from your profile settings. Removal is permanent and cannot be undone.

4. Third Party Services

Spendwise relies on a small number of trusted third-party providers to operate, such as hosting infrastructure and transactional email delivery. These providers receive only the information needed to perform their function and are contractually obligated to protect your data.

We do not embed third-party advertising or analytics trackers that build a profile of your activity across other websites.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can review and update your account information at any time from your profile settings. You may export your expense data and you may delete your account entirely. If you have questions about the data we hold about you, please reach out using the contact details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, our practices, or applicable law. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will make a reasonable effort to notify you, for example by highlighting the change in the app or sending a notice to your registered email.

Your continued use of Spendwise after the updated policy takes effect constitutes your acceptance of the changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We take privacy questions seriously and will respond as quickly as we can.
